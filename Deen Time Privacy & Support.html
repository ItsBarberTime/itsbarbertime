Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

In short

Deen Time has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings and progress stay on your phone. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location, used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction on the device.
  • Your settings: prayer guide, school, translation language, athan and reminder choices.
  • Your progress: lessons and chapters read, starred duas, and Quran memorization.
  • Prayer notifications, which are scheduled on the phone itself.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What is sent to other services, and why

Some features need to look something up online. Only the information needed for that request is sent, at the moment you use the feature. We do not receive or keep it.

Like any website, these services can see your device’s internet (IP) address when they answer a request. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

Permissions

  • Location – only while you use the app, for prayer times, Qibla and nearby places.
  • Camera – only when you open the scanner, to read barcodes. No photos are saved or sent.
  • Notifications – for the athan and the daily reminder you choose.

You can turn any of these off at any time in your phone’s Settings.

Children

Deen Time does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

My prayer times look wrong

Check that location is allowed for Deen Time. If you follow the Hanafi school, set Settings → School to Hanafi for the later Asr time. You can also change the calculation method in Settings → Prayer time calculation.

The athan doesn’t play

Make sure notifications are allowed for Deen Time and that each prayer is switched on in Settings → Notifications & Athan. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch and Focus modes can silence notification sounds. Open the app every few days so it can schedule the next week of prayers.

Mosques or halal food won’t load

These come from OpenStreetMap, which is free and run by volunteers. Check your internet connection and tap Try again. Some areas have fewer places listed – you can add missing mosques and restaurants at openstreetmap.org.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Deen Time. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area.

Is the content checked?

The lessons follow mainstream Sunni scholarship and give their Quran and hadith sources. If you find a mistake, please email us so it can be corrected.
